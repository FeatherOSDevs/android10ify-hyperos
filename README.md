# android10ify-hyperos
A simple guide to make HyperOS look and feel like Android 10 — clean, minimal, nostalgic.

## 📱 HyperOS to Android 10 Style – Modding Guide
This project is a step-by-step guide to transform Xiaomi's HyperOS into a clean, minimal, and nostalgic Android 10 look.  
Whether you're a fan of the old AOSP style or just want a lightweight and distraction-free experience, this repo provides the tools, apps, and tweaks to get you there.

---

### 🔧 What’s Included:
- **Launcher and icon pack suggestions** (Pixel/AOSP style)
- **System UI adjustments** (fonts, colors, control center tweaks)
- **Optional APKs and themes** to enhance the Android 10 look
- **Screenshots and comparisons** (before/after)
- **Extra resources** to make it as close to Android 10 as possible

⚠️ **Note:** This guide does not require root, and focuses on safe, reversible modifications. Perfect for customization lovers who miss the classic Android vibe.

## 🔑 First of all, you'll need:

- An **HyperOS Device** (Mi, Redmi, Poco, etc.)
- **Lawnchair legacy**
- **"Pixel - Icon Pack"** For general icons
- **Zedge** (optional for sounds)
- These themes:
  - **"A13 Ultra"** For boot animation
  - **"Pixel experience"** For system icons

---

### 🔽 Download the Themes:

- [A13 Ultra Boot Animation](https://www.mtzfile.pw/detail/d5952bd8-7a22-4179-bfce-fdc135053ff4?srsltid=AfmBOopCIG-R4tZ_zknQxszorz8BlcMg7LAkmgv-w8gG7jYnGpIAypoF#gsc.tab=0)
- [Pixel Experience MIUI Theme](https://www.miuithemer.com/themes/pixel-experience-miui-theme-for-xiaomi-and-redmi-devices/)

---

### ⚙️ Applying the Themes:

1. **Go to the Themes app and apply**:
    - **Pixel Experience** for icons
    - **A13 Ultra** for the boot animation

2. To change the boot animation:
   - Go to the **Themes app**, tap the **user icon**, then select "Customize Theme".
   - Select **Boot Animation** and choose **A13 Ultra**.

---

### 🔄 Changing the Launcher:

1. Go to the **Settings app** and search for:  
   - **Default Apps**
2. Tap on **Launcher**, and select **Lawnchair** as your default launcher.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/90c90870-2d06-49e8-81ec-cab57d2b6409" alt="screenshot" width="400"/>

---

### 🔧 Customizing Lawnchair:

1. **Long-press the home screen** and select **"Settings"**.
2. Navigate to **"Theme"**.
   - If you haven’t already, download the **Pixel Q Icon Pack**:  
   [Pixel Q Icon Pack](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ru.pt.iconpack.pixel_q&hl=es_419)

3. In the **Theme** section, select **Icon Pack** and choose **Pixel - Icon Pack**.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9409c0c4-622f-49f9-a9e4-992935014b76" alt="screenshot" width="400"/>

4. **Change icon shape** to "Circle" (by default, it's set to square).
5. You can set up the **"At a glance" widget** by long-pressing it on the home screen.

---

### 🎉 Final Result:
Now, you should have a fully customized Android 10-like experience on your HyperOS device!
